About Us

Bridging Theory & Social Impact

The IMPACT Project is a premier, student-led consulting chapter at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world execution by providing pro-bono consulting services to startups, corporates, and NGOs. We drive positive change for our clients while cultivating future leadership talent.

FOUNDED 2021

Our DNA: Actionable Strategy

Founded in 2021 at Sri Venkateswara College (Delhi University), The IMPACT Project is an elite student consultancy. We assemble multidisciplinary cohorts of the college's brightest analytical minds to deliver pro-bono strategic advisory services. We transform academic rigor into high-impact, real-world growth solutions.

HYBRID MODEL

Dual Engagement Model: Corporate Startups & Social NGOs

We balance corporate value generation with social capital scaling. By advising corporate startups, we gain sharp market exposure and GTM strategy experience. We then direct these strategies towards NGOs and social welfare organizations to scale grants, manage budgets, and structure community drives.

Our Consulting Specializations

Empirical Research

Data-backed market diagnostics, primary surveys, attitudinal studies, competitive mapping, and microeconomic analysis.

Corporate Strategy

Go-To-Market (GTM) launches, influencer marketing designs, platform onboarding assessments, and UI/UX conversion optimizations.

Capital Scaling

Financial allocation models, volunteer acquisition strategies, crowdfunding trust mechanics, and government grant roadmaps.
