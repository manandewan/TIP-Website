About The Chapter

Bridging Academic Theory with High-Impact Advisory.

Founded in 2021 at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, The IMPACT Project (Implementing Peace Action Change Together) is an elite student-led consulting chapter. We equip high-potential corporate startups, social enterprises, and grassroots NGOs with data-driven, actionable solutions—measuring success by lives improved, not just slides delivered.

FOUNDED 2021 • DELHI UNIVERSITY

We assemble multidisciplinary cohorts of Sri Venkateswara College’s sharpest analytical minds across Commerce, Economics, Statistics, and Sciences. Guided by academic rigor and industry mentorship, our consultants turn complex market dynamics into clear, execution-ready roadmaps.
